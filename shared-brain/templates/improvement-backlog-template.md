## Discipline

- Append only; do not rewrite or delete prior rows. Mark a superseded row rather than removing it.
- Cite real evidence (task ID, investigation slug, file, session) for every row.
- Keep this file client-specific — do not copy findings here into another project's backlog;
  cross-project reuse goes through the Shared Brain promotion path instead.
